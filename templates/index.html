01 — THE IDEA

From a city’s
coffee ritual
to a new material.

CAFÉLIA is designed as an integrated circular-manufacturing project: recover spent coffee grounds, cultivate mycelium, form a bio-composite leather sheet, and use qualified offcuts for smaller products.

The proposed facility is located in Tan Phu Trung Industrial Park, Ho Chi Minh City, with a 5,000 m² factory and a one-way production flow separating biological cultivation from mechanical finishing.

02 — CLOSED-LOOP PROCESS

Five stages.
One circular flow.

The proposed production system combines biological cultivation with precision mechanical finishing.

01

Recover

Fresh spent coffee grounds are collected from F&B partners and delivered for rapid stabilization.

02

Prepare

Screening, drying, grinding, nutrient blending and high-pressure sterilization create a controlled substrate.

03

Grow

Pleurotus ostreatus mycelium is cultivated in controlled clean-room conditions for dense biological growth.

04

Form

The harvested mycelium sheet is dried, biologically inactivated, reinforced and thermally pressed.

05

Craft

Finished material becomes B2B rolls or accessories; qualified offcuts are redirected into Brand Charms.

The project plans to retain and train 25 local sewing workers while developing technical capability for laser cutting, embroidery, finishing and quality control.

06 — BUSINESS MODEL

Built for

Industrial partners need material consistency and technical support. Consumers need design, function and a credible story. CAFÉLIA is structured to serve both.

Bio-leather rolls, Swatch & OEM Kits, material trials, customization and OEM/ODM cooperation.

B2B2C

Customized accessories, branded packaging and tone-on-tone embroidery for organizations and events.
